# Gale Force - Requirements, User Stories and Session Walkthrough

**Status:** Draft v1 - design only
**Companion to:** [SPEC.md](./SPEC.md). Priorities: **P0** first playable (headless + greybox); **P1** first complete game; **P2** later.

## 1. User stories and acceptance scenarios

### US-1 (P0) - Sail a time trial
As a sailor, I sail a windward-leeward course as fast as I can and compare with a ghost.
1. **Given** a seeded course, **when** I start, **then** I see wind, instruments, marks and the start line.
2. **Given** I sail within 10° of the no-go zone, **then** the sails luff and speed drops according to the model.
3. **Given** I tack, **then** I lose speed in a manner dependent on my timing and heel; a well-executed roll tack loses less.
4. **Given** I finish, **then** I see elapsed time, penalties and a replay with a ghost comparison.

### US-2 (P0) - Wind shifts and puffs matter
1. **Given** an oscillating wind scenario, **when** I tack on a header versus on a lift, **then** the finishing time difference is consistent with the model (S7).
2. **Given** a puff, **then** its approach is visible on the water and its effect on boat speed and heel is physically consistent.

### US-3 (P0) - Replay and verify
Replays re-simulate to identical finish times; tampered logs are rejected.

### US-4 (P1) - Race a fleet with rules enforced
1. **Given** two boats on a collision course on opposite tacks, **then** the port-tack boat must keep clear; failure produces a penalty and a logged ruling with the geometric facts.
2. **Given** an overlap at the mark zone, **then** mark-room is applied per the implemented rule logic.
3. **Given** I am over the line at the start gun, **then** I am notified and must restart.

### US-5 (P1) - Match race a rival
Best-of-N match with pre-start maneuvers; penalties by rule.

### US-6 (P1) - Daily course and leaderboard
Seeded wind/course/class; ranked by time plus penalties; verified by re-simulation.

### US-7 (P1) - Learn
Tutorial: points of sail, trim, tacking, laylines, starts, rules basics. Assist levels from Cadet to Expert.

### US-8 (P1) - Review with a tactical coach
Post-race analysis: VMG, shifts, decisions, rule events. An optional AI coach explains key moments with citations to timestamps.

### US-9 (P1) - Broadcast replay
Free camera, broadcast director camera, telemetry overlays.

### US-10 (P2) - Live fleet and team racing; offshore real-weather mode

## 2. Functional requirements

### Simulation
- **FR-001 (P0)** Deterministic: same build + scenario + inputs ⇒ identical states and finish times.
- **FR-002 (P0)** 6-DOF boat model: aerodynamic sail forces from CL/CD curves; hull resistance; appendage lift/drag with stall; righting moment with hiking; roll/pitch damping; current.
- **FR-003 (P0)** Apparent wind computed from true wind and boat velocity at each step.
- **FR-004 (P0)** Wind field: mean + shifts (oscillating and persistent) + advected gust field + shear; deterministic from the seed.
- **FR-005 (P1)** Geographic wind effects and wind shadow behind boats.
- **FR-006 (P0)** Ocean: spectral wave model with a deterministic CPU sampler; visuals from the same parameters.
- **FR-007 (P0)** Controls: helm, mainsheet, and class-appropriate trim; crew actions with physical effects.
- **FR-008 (P1)** Assist levels change automation but not the physics.
- **FR-009 (P0)** Boat speed polars generated by the model fall within tolerance of published reference data for the class (S1).

### Rules and racing
- **FR-020 (P1)** Rules engine implements the staged subset (port/starboard, windward/leeward, overlap, mark-room, start rules, tacking, contact avoidance, penalty turns).
- **FR-021 (P1)** Every ruling is logged with the geometric facts that triggered it.
- **FR-022 (P1)** Courses are generated deterministically with legal marks and line bias.
- **FR-023 (P1)** Scoring: low-point series, match-race scoring, time-plus-penalty.

### Competition and data
- **FR-030 (P0)** Replay format and scenario format follow platform DATA_CONTRACTS.
- **FR-031 (P1)** Server re-simulation verifies leaderboard submissions.
- **FR-032 (P1)** Ratings by format and class (Glicko-2/TrueSkill).

### AI (see [ML.md](./ML.md), [AGENTS.md](./AGENTS.md))
- **FR-040 (P0)** Scripted polar-following opponents with tactical rules.
- **FR-041 (P1)** Learned tactical sailors trained in the headless sim with published evaluation.
- **FR-042 (P1)** Optional coach agent is replay-grounded and optional.

## 3. Non-functional requirements
- **NFR-001** ≥ 60 FPS at 1080p on the Reference tier with the full ocean (target; S4).
- **NFR-002** Headless sim ≥ 200× real time for a 10-boat fleet on the Dev machine.
- **NFR-003** A race lasts 5-20 minutes.
- **NFR-004** Single-player offline.
- **NFR-005** Physics parameters and hull data cite sources or are labelled as tunable design values.
- **NFR-006** Rule logic does not reproduce rule text.

## 4. Session walkthrough (Fleet race, ~15 minutes)

| Time | Experience | Systems |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Briefing: "Daily Course: windward-leeward, 4 legs, 12-knot northwesterly oscillating ±12° every 8 min, 0.4 m chop, outgoing tide 0.5 kt." Choose assist level. | Scenario |
| 1:00 | Pre-start: 8 AI boats circle. Player studies the line bias via instrument and compass. | Course, instruments |
| 3:00 | Start: player takes a port-end start, accelerates, hikes in the building breeze. | FR-002, FR-007 |
| 5:00 | Header detected on the instruments; player tacks. A starboard-tack AI boat approaches: player ducks astern. | FR-020 |
| 7:00 | A dark patch on the water ahead: puff. Heel increases; player eases the sheet and hikes harder. | FR-004 |
| 9:00 | Windward mark: boat overlap inside the zone; mark-room awarded; ruling logged. | FR-020/021 |
| 11:00 | Downwind: gybe in puff. A boat to windward casts wind shadow; player breaks the cover. | FR-005 |
| 14:30 | Finish in 3rd. A 720° penalty turn earlier cost two places (ruling explained). | FR-023 |
| 15:00 | Debrief: shift graph, decisions, rule events; optional coach reviews tacking decisions. | US-8 |
